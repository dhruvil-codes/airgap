# Community code of conduct

Everyone participating in airgap's issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces is expected to make collaboration respectful and useful.

- Discuss the work with specific, constructive feedback. Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience. Avoid insults, harassment, discrimination and sexualized content.
- Respect privacy. Do not share someone else's personal information, credentials or private communications without permission.
- Follow the project's security reporting process and respect other people's systems and cloud accounts.

Report conduct concerns privately to [dhruvil@bydhruvil.in](mailto:dhruvil@bydhruvil.in). Include links or context when safe to do so. Reports will be handled with discretion; confidentiality may be limited when action is needed to protect others.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close a discussion, restrict participation or block an account. Responses will consider the severity and history of the behavior. These expectations apply to maintainers as well as contributors.
